## 2. Long Descriptions

### English (Play Store Long Description)
Welcome to **Shusto** — your ultimate digital healthcare and telehealth companion in Bangladesh. Designed to bring quality medical services right to your fingertips, Shusto connects you with experienced doctors, certified diagnostic labs, 24/7 emergency ambulance services, and trusted pharmacies instantly.

🌟 **Key Features of Shusto:**
- 🩺 **Online Doctor Consultation:** Connect with verified specialist doctors via video call, audio, or live chat for expert medical advice.
- 🧪 **Home Lab Test Booking:** Book pathology and diagnostic tests with sample collection from the comfort of your home. Reports delivered directly in the app.
- 🚑 **24/7 Emergency Ambulance:** Instant access to emergency ambulance services across Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet, Khulna, Rajshahi, Barishal, Rangpur, Mymensingh, and Sherpur.
- 💊 **Medicine Store & Pharmacy Delivery:** Order genuine prescription medicines and healthcare products with fast home delivery.
- 💳 **Secure Digital Wallet & Cashback:** Manage your healthcare funds safely with built-in wallet support and transparent transaction history.
- 🔒 **Privacy & Account Management:** Full control over your medical records with robust privacy protection and 31-day secure account recovery/deletion policy.
- 🌐 **Bilingual Support:** Seamlessly switch between Bengali and English anytime.

Whether you need routine medical care, emergency assistance, or specialist consultations, Shusto is committed to keeping you and your family healthy and safe. Download Shusto today!
